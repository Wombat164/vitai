- The rollup's `## Coverage` line subtracts a stated absence only from the count
  it is about. #428 shipped it subtracting ANY stated absence, which was
  indistinguishable from this over every fixture that then existed because only
  `weight.kg` was ever declared absent; the rows added here made the two rules
  differ, and the first one read `daily: 82` where the athlete had lived and
  logged 84 days - one field of forty carrying a reason cost the whole day.
  Contract 51's own words settle it: it permits "a day that happened, a weigh-in
  ATTEMPTED, a session recorded, with a field missing from it". A weigh-in is
  attempted for the sake of one number, so a weigh-in with no `kg` is not one; a
  day and a session are the observation themselves. `readings` now takes the
  field the count is about, and takes nothing for the two datasets where the row
  is the observation.
