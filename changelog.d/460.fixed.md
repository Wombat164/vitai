- Two comments in `verdicts.py` and one line of the uncertainty roadmap called
  the pre-registered 1.74 a "95 per cent half-width". The ratio table that
  produced it defines it as `u_rate / half-band`, a standard uncertainty - so
  the 95 per cent half-width is about 3.4 half-bands and the mislabel
  understated the finding by a factor of two.
