- The rollup's `## Coverage` line counts readings rather than rows, so a weigh-in
  the record states was not performed is no longer counted as one. The section
  rendered three list lengths, and the two rows this release adds to the demo
  turned `weight: 62` into `weight: 64` - a figure that did not change meaning by
  a little but changed from one fact to a different one, silently, on a record
  the athlete is shown. Contract 51's own rule is that a reason explains a hole
  and never fills one, and a coverage figure claims how much of the window the
  record actually observed; the engine was the consumer reading the reason as a
  value. No new policy: the demo's line reads `weight: 62` again, and it is a
  reading count on both sides of the change.
- Scoped to EXPLAINED absence at first, and the fuller shape - observed,
  explained-absent and silent reported separately - has since landed beside it;
  see the `Added` entry for this issue. Measured across the demo and all fifteen
  personas before choosing: `absent_fields` is written on four rows, every one of
  them `weight.kg`, and no weight row anywhere carries a null `kg` without one,
  which is why no shipped number moved at either step.
- `weight.absent_fields` leaves `test_field_population.UNREAD`, and the pair that
  entry used to cover splits. Which fields are absent now has a consumer;
  `weight.absent_reason` does not, and its block is real rather than inherited -
  every consumer of the reason grades holes against each other, and that ranking
  is blocked on evidence no record holds. The register measures a read as the key
  appearing in a consumer and `schema` is never credited as one, so the coverage
  count names the key rather than only calling `schema.absent_fields`, which is
  what makes the read one the register can see.
- `readings` counts rows that CARRY the field rather than rows that do not say
  they are absent. The two were indistinguishable while no fixture had a silent
  hole, and the first synthetic one showed the cost: four weigh-ins holding two
  readings, one explained absence and one silent rendered as `weight: 3` above a
  line reporting one unexplained hole - two numbers on one record that cannot
  both be true. A hole is not an observation whether or not it is accounted for,
  and the invariant is now checkable and checked: observed plus explained plus
  unexplained is every row.
