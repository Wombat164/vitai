- **What each contract TOUCHED, declared per contract so a client can answer
  "do I need to move" without a human reading prose** (#451, from the
  measurement in #450). `semantics/contract_impact.toml` names the surfaces
  each contract from 47 onward added, widened or narrowed; a client names what
  it reads; the verdict is the intersection. `vitai contract-impact --since N
  --reads <surfaces>` is the CI step (exit 0 stay, 1 move, 2 cannot answer),
  `api.contract_impact()` is the same capability as a method, and the whole
  declaration rides in `vitai schema --json` under `impact`.
- **There is deliberately no "must a client move" field.** That question has
  one safe answer, `yes`, which is never wrong and costs the author nothing,
  so a field shaped like it stops carrying information within a month. It
  could not carry contract 48 in any case: no column moved, and a client
  matching `kind` exhaustively silently drops every `band` row.
- **Five gates keep it from rotting**: a contract from the floor that declares
  nothing fails; a contract declaring an EMPTY list fails, because a bump that
  touched no published surface should not have happened; a surface that
  resolves to no table, meta key or report method fails; a declaration that
  omits what `db.py`'s own contract note names fails; and a change kind no
  contract uses fails. Each was verified by breaking the declaration and
  watching its own gate fire.
- No contract move. `CONTRACT_VERSION` is untouched: no table or column
  changes shape, and `schema()` gaining a key is additive.
