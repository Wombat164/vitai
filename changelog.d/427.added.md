- The demo exercises all six `absent_reason` codes, across all three datasets
  that permit a stated absence. It wrote one of six before this, so a client
  could render every one of them as the same grey square and pass every job it
  had - and that is what happened: a downstream reader absorbed contract 51 and
  dropped every row whose only content was an absence, correct against its
  fixtures and wrong about the record. The rule the corpus was missing is not
  "more coverage" but that a field the engine publishes must appear in the
  artifact every conformance client reads, in every form it can take.
- Each code sits where the record earns it, and the record says so on the row.
  `unable-to-obtain`: the athlete stood on the scale on 2030-06-25 and the
  display never woke - attempted, and no value came back, which is the
  distinction from the travel week where nothing was attempted. `error`: the
  first morning home it read 71.2 kg against 76 either side, having been moved
  onto the bath mat, and the record refuses a number it holds rather than
  missing one. `asked-declined` and `asked-unknown` need a question and an
  answer, so they go on `daily`: the clinic intake on the second DEXA day asked
  about alcohol and the athlete left it blank, and the weekly review asked how
  the hotel week slept and the athlete did not know. `not-applicable`: lifting
  covers no distance. `not-performed` on a session: the erg records work and
  nobody wore a heart-rate strap - deliberately not framed as the console being
  unable to read one, which is contract 44's `competence: absent` and is why
  `schema` dropped FHIR's `unsupported` code.
- `test_a_vocabulary_field_shows_more_than_one_of_its_values` now checks every
  closed vocabulary instead of one. It had been scoped to `daily.coverage`
  since #204 while its own docstring said it was "stated in the general form
  for the vocabularies that follow" - and forty followed. The sites are derived
  from `schema.py` rather than listed, so a new vocabulary field enters the
  measurement by being written; a call the derivation cannot attribute to a
  dataset raises rather than dropping out of it.
- Measured, which #427 recorded as an open question rather than a number:
  **41 vocabulary-governed fields, of which 9 carried fewer than two values
  across the whole corpus** and 15 across the demo. Three were `absent_reason`
  and are fixed here. The remaining six are registered in `VOCAB_SINGLE` with
  reasons that must commit to A DECISION or A GAP, and four of them are one
  fact: `AUTHORS` distinguishes athlete from coach and no record in this corpus
  has a coach.
- `daily.absent_fields` / `daily.absent_reason` and the `sessions` pair leave
  `UNWRITTEN`. That entry said the two cases were the OUTAGE shape and belonged
  to contract 49's work, which was true of the outage case and was read as
  covering the whole field. The codes written here are the ones no instrument
  can produce, none needs the false-zero machinery, and the outage day is still
  unwritten and still belongs to that work.
- `weight.note` leaves the demo field register. It was held out because a
  sentence beside a reading is "prose about a number rather than the number",
  which stops being true on a row that carries no reading: `absent_reason:
  error` can say the class of failure and cannot say the scale had been moved,
  and contract 51 forbids parking the rejected value in `kg`.
