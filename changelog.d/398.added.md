- **Two question kinds for an instrument that stopped** (#398, contract 49).
  `questions()` gains `outage` - a source that had been contributing and has
  gone quiet - and `false_zero` - a day a source reported an exact zero it had
  never reported before. From a real outage: a charger left at home, a watch
  that fell silent, and one day on its last charge reporting an athlete who
  had been moving as `steps: 0`.
- **The false zero is the one that costs.** A gap announces itself and every
  reader already treats absence as absence. A zero averages into weekly steps,
  `kcal_out` and energy availability as though somebody observed it, and the
  athlete will never report it because from their side nothing looks wrong.
- **Two kinds rather than one list**, because they want opposite gestures. A
  gap is answered by an APPEND; a false zero by a SUPERSEDE, since a row
  exists and is wrong, and appending beside it leaves the lie in the record
  with a correction next to it rather than over it. `kind` carries that with
  nothing to re-derive, and no parallel `remedy` field is emitted - it would
  co-vary with `kind` in every row forever.
- **Neither rule carries a number.** Silence is measured against the longest
  gap that source has already shown in this record, so a weekly source is not
  silent after two days and a daily one is. A source seen once is never asked
  why it stopped, and one seen twice has an anecdote rather than a cadence:
  "longer than ever before" against a single observation would call a source
  silent on its second quiet day. One question per run, and per source only
  the run still open at the viewpoint.
- **The field set for `false_zero` is declared, and measurement forced that.**
  The rule was built first over every numeric daily field, on the issue's own
  reasoning that a zero out of family is recognisable from the distribution.
  Against the shipped corpus it produced four questions, every one a false
  positive - three first-ordinary-day `pain` zeros and one `sugar_g` - and no
  true positive anywhere. A second attempt keyed on "the source writes this
  field every day it appears" separated nothing, because in this corpus `pain`
  is written on 71 of 71 days. Whether a zero means NOT OBSERVED or means NONE
  is a fact about what a field means, and no view of its distribution recovers
  it. `ZERO_MEANS_UNOBSERVED` holds the one field where the claim is safe.
- The near-floor `kcal_out` the outage also produced is **not** detected and
  is not claimed to be: separating "near the floor" from "low" needs a
  threshold on a distribution.
