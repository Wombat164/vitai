- **Presence in the variation-floor register is not the finding; run length
  is** (#462). `kenji` was built to carry published day-to-day variation and
  joins `BELOW_THE_FLOOR` anyway, with one run of six days. Rebuilding his
  series under 200 seeds, 143 of them - 72 per cent - hold at least one
  stretch of five days or more under the declared 0.2 kg floor, with a median
  longest run of five days and a longest seen of nine. A realistic daily
  series of this length is more likely than not to trip that floor somewhere,
  so a record's absence from the register says less than its position in it:
  `vera` at 71 days and `hana` at 26 cannot fluctuate, `nora` at six is a real
  flat fortnight, and he belongs with `nora`. The floor is unchanged - this
  records what it measures, which is narrower than its name.
