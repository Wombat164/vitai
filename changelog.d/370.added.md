- A `crossings` dataset: round-number and personal-first milestones,
  goal-independent and history-wide - the two thirds of #370 that need no
  height. "You broke 80 kg" and "that is your lowest ever" are true or false
  of the weight series alone, whether or not a goal was ever declared, and
  `milestones`' columns all assume a declared goal and a scoring bucket - so
  this is a new table rather than a fake goal and fraction forced into that
  one. `round_number` mints on crossing a multiple of 5 kg in either
  direction, a CHOICE of ladder and not a derivation from anything about a
  kilogram. `personal_first` mints on a reading that is the lowest or highest
  the record has ever seen at the point it was taken; the very first reading
  in a record mints nothing, since it is trivially both and has no prior
  evidence to cite. Every row carries the evidence pair the design asks for -
  `previous_value`/`previous_date`, the reading that establishes the OTHER
  side of the crossing - and re-crossing the same round number later mints a
  new row with its own pair rather than being suppressed as a repeat. Reads
  the CANONICAL weight series, never raw claims, the same reason `milestones`
  does: a day resolved from two competing sources must count once. `vitai
  crossings`, `Vitai.crossings()` and the matching `crossings` agent tool.
- The band crossing (BMI) is deliberately NOT in this change - it needs a
  height, and whether the engine may ever publish the band's NAME is a
  separate, still-open ruling recorded on the issue.
- `height_cm` joins `MEASUREMENT_KINDS`, beside `waist_cm` and `hip_cm`,
  decided over a `[physiology]` setting in `vitai.toml`: a height in a
  mutable config file is #148's defect one measurand over, where a value
  entered once at forty would silently rewrite every later computation
  against a record that runs to seventy. Dated and correctable like every
  other reading here, and nothing in this change reads it yet.
- Fixed: `round_number`'s evidence pair named the wrong reading. It shipped
  as the reading immediately BEFORE the crossing, which cannot support the
  sentence the issue asks for - #370's own worked example shows "lowest
  ever" and "first time below 80 in over a year" both false of a real
  record, with "first below 80 since February" the true, smaller claim, and
  the immediately preceding reading is evidence for none of the three.
  `previous_value`/`previous_date` now name the most recent reading already
  on the DESTINATION side of the level - below it for a downward crossing,
  above it for an upward one - found by searching the series, not by
  reading the one row before this one. A level never reached from that side
  before now yields a null pair, which is a stronger fact ("first time
  ever"), not a missing one. `personal_first` is unchanged: its evidence is
  the running extreme itself, which was already the right fact.
