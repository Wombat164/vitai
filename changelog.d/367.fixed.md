- **`vitai init` now stamps a `[device]` section.** `[targets]`, `[tripwires]`,
  `[preferences]`, `[resolution]` and `[inference]` all shipped with their keys
  spelled out in comments; `[device]` - the setting that is the entire reason a
  second machine does not collide with the first - was absent, not even as a
  commented example. The TOML shape was discoverable only by reading
  `config.py`. Stamping over the template into an existing repo does not
  retroactively add it; the section only reaches a repo initialised after this
  change, or one an athlete edits by hand.
- Checked whether the collision itself - two unslugged machines both appending
  to the plain `<dataset>.jsonl` - is detectable after the fact, and it is not
  without guessing: an unslugged writer never stamps `device` on the row, so a
  second unslugged writer produces rows indistinguishable in shape from the
  first. `devices.py` now says so, rather than leaving the question open.
