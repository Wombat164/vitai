- The rollup's `## Coverage` section reports what the record did NOT observe,
  and whether it said why. #428 called this the only answer that gives an
  athlete a reason to fill a gap in, and that is the argument: a morning written
  down as one they did not weigh is a decision, and a morning simply not there
  is a gap they can still fill. One number for both told them nothing they could
  act on. The demo now reads `4 weigh-ins record no reading and say why; none is
  unexplained.`
- Silent when there is nothing to report, which is `over_days`' rule in the same
  module and for the same reason: a phrase on every rollup is a phrase nobody
  reads, and the marked minority is legible precisely because the unmarked
  majority is quiet. No adjective and no threshold either - the engine has no
  published basis for where patchy begins.
- Nothing here reads `absent_reason`. `absent_fields` names the field, which is
  all the split needs; every consumer of the reason ranks holes against each
  other, and that ranking is #402's band question and stays blocked. Counting
  explained apart from silent ranks nothing.
- Scoped to `weight`, and the other two say why rather than printing two zeroes
  forever: a weigh-in is attempted for the sake of one number, so a weigh-in
  without it is a hole, while a day and a session ARE the observation. The
  equivalent for `daily` is a day with no row at all, which is a calendar
  measurement needing a window - and inventing one here is the cutoff with no
  published basis this module already refuses twice.
