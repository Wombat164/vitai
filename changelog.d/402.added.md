- `absent_fields` and `absent_reason` on the observation datasets (contract
  51): why a value is missing, not just that it is. A null said only that
  nothing is there, and "nobody measured it", "it was measured and rejected",
  "the athlete was asked and declined", "asked and they do not know" and "it
  does not apply here" are different facts with opposite consequences for a
  reader.
- The other half of contract 49. `false_zero` exists because a dying watch
  reported a zero for a day the athlete spent moving; retiring that zero leaves
  a hole, and without a reason the next reader re-derives the same confusion
  from the same silence.
- A reason explains a hole and never fills one. A reason beside a value that is
  present is refused at validation rather than silently preferred, because that
  row makes two claims and offers no way to choose between them.
- The vocabulary takes FHIR `dataAbsentReason`'s distinctions and not its
  codelist. `unknown` and `not-asked` are dropped because a null
  `absent_reason` already says exactly that; `unsupported` because contract
  44's `competence: absent` already carries it; the numeric sentinels because
  smuggling a value through a type is the disease contract 49 cured.
- **`comparability` has a writer, and a pipeline that fills it** (#402).
  `comparability` (contract 46) is the dataset holding a MEASURED overlap
  between two instruments - the only route by which an error band can be
  earned rather than assumed - and no record anywhere had ever written a row.
  The rule said earn it and there was nothing to earn it from.
- **`calibration.overlap_calibration`** reads two origins' claims for one
  field over the dates both covered and reports what the record actually saw:
  a median difference, the full observed width, the two tails, and the days it
  had to drop. On `Vitai.overlap_calibration` and as `vitai calibrate`.
- **Bias and spread stay two facts.** A median says which way the pair leans;
  a spread says how far apart they got. `vera`, the persona shipped with this,
  has a median difference of 0.00 km and a range of 1.26 km - the two agree on
  average and disagree on any run under tree cover - and a design collapsing
  them into one number would report that pair as agreeing.
- **The asymmetry does not fit in the row, and the engine says so.** `bias`
  and `spread` are each a single number, so a range running further one way
  than the other cannot be written down. The tails are returned beside the row
  rather than folded into it, and both the API and the CLI say that a
  consumer must not read `bias` plus or minus half the `spread`. **#402 has to
  decide whether `comparability` gains a field before a band can rest on this.**
- **The derivation never proposes `comparable`.** That status LIFTS the seam
  refusal, and no overlap can establish on its own that two instruments are
  interchangeable. An earlier version decided the status from the arithmetic
  and called a pair comparable when the median came out at zero - which `vera`
  produces across instruments differing by over a kilometre on a given run.
  A measured difference of zero is now reported as an offset of zero. The
  engine measures; the record declares.
- **Nothing is corrected.** Measuring that two instruments disagree is not a
  licence to adjust either, there is deliberately no function that could, and
  a test pins that the canonical distance for a two-claim day is one of the two
  claims rather than an adjusted or averaged third number.
- **No number is invented.** An overlap of fewer than three paired readings
  produces no row: two differences are the observations themselves rather than
  a statement about the pair. A day where either origin recorded twice is
  dropped as ambiguous and counted, because an overlap that is thin because
  half of it could not be paired is a different fact from one that was thin.
