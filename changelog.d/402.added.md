- `absent_fields` and `absent_reason` on the observation datasets (contract
  51): why a value is missing, not just that it is. A null said only that
  nothing is there, and "nobody measured it", "it was measured and rejected",
  "the athlete was asked and declined", "asked and they do not know" and "it
  does not apply here" are different facts with opposite consequences for a
  reader.
- The other half of contract 49. `false_zero` exists because a dying watch
  reported a zero for a day the athlete spent moving; retiring that zero leaves
  a hole, and without a reason the next reader re-derives the same confusion
  from the same silence.
- A reason explains a hole and never fills one. A reason beside a value that is
  present is refused at validation rather than silently preferred, because that
  row makes two claims and offers no way to choose between them.
- The vocabulary takes FHIR `dataAbsentReason`'s distinctions and not its
  codelist. `unknown` and `not-asked` are dropped because a null
  `absent_reason` already says exactly that; `unsupported` because contract
  44's `competence: absent` already carries it; the numeric sentinels because
  smuggling a value through a type is the disease contract 49 cured.
