- A negated verb no longer hides a category claim. `_disclaimed` suppressed a
  match when the clause before it carried a negation token, and `never` is one -
  which is right for "vitai does not classify anyone as obese" and wrong for
  "tom and rachel never leave the obese band at any recorded weight". The second
  negates the LEAVING and asserts the membership harder than the bare form does,
  and it is a category applied to two named people in a public repo.
- That sentence is the instance #387 wrote and #388 could not reach: it reported
  nothing in ANY scanned file, so widening the surface from eleven entries to the
  whole tree did not touch it. Two changes, two holes; this is the one in the
  matcher. #388's control carried a reworded verb and a test pinning the gap,
  which said to delete it the day this landed. Deleted, and the control now
  carries the historical wording verbatim.
- Scoped to CATEGORY, and the other two families keep their negation. A care
  directive and a purpose claim are things the SYSTEM does, so negating them is a
  genuine disclaimer - and the commonest one here is load-bearing: "It is not
  intended to identify, monitor, explain, treat or compensate for any disease"
  appears in README.md, the doctrine and the wiki. A category is not something
  the system does, it is something said ABOUT a person, so the negation lands on
  a different verb and cannot be read the same way.
- Measured before choosing, which is what the issue asked for. Dropping the
  disclaimer for category over the whole 424-file surface costs TWO findings,
  both in `tests/test_boundary_gate.py` and both the quoting shape the register
  already holds fourteen of. The alternative - narrowing the negation to clauses
  carrying a saying-verb - fires on the regulatory sentence above in three files,
  which is a matcher tuned until the fixture passes.
- A disclaimer using a category word is still writeable and now has to be
  registered rather than inferred: "vitai does not classify anyone as obese" is a
  finding, and saying otherwise is a decision somebody records. That is the
  fail-closed direction.
