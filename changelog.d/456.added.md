- `schema()["absent_reasons"]` and `schema()["absence"]`, with `vitai absence`:
  the one vocabulary a client has to WRITE, published with what tells its words
  apart - whether the athlete was asked, whether asking again adds anything,
  and whether the absence counts as a gap at all. Six words without those
  distinctions would leave a client doing what it does today, which is
  spelling them.
- The dropped FHIR `dataAbsentReason` codes are published with the reason each
  was dropped, so a client reading that codelist beside this one is not left
  wondering whether an omission was a decision.
