- `vitai outlook` and `Vitai.weight_outlook()`: what this record's own weight
  has done over an elapsed interval - the median change over `d` days, the
  tenth and ninetieth percentiles of those changes, and the two counts behind
  them. Nothing is modelled and no physiological model is involved; the
  figures are order statistics of readings the record holds.
- Each horizon states both how many `d`-day pairs the series holds and how
  many DISJOINT `d`-day stretches it observed. The second is the coverage that
  gates: overlapping pairs are not a sample size, and a gap cannot flatter a
  tiled count the way `span // days` can.
- A protocol change or an undeclared instrument change under the series
  refuses the outlook, the same two refusals `weight_rate` already makes.
