- An `instruments` dataset: the entity behind the identity `origin` already
  names, over an interval. Contract 44 gave that string capabilities and said
  a register would later give it an entity; this is that register. `vitai
  instruments`, `Vitai.instrument(origin, on)` and the matching agent tools
  answer which physical thing reported under a name on a date.
- The join is `(origin, date)`, never `origin` alone. "My watch" in 2026 and
  "my watch" in 2030 are different objects, so a lookup on the identity would
  attribute every historical reading to whatever reports under that name now.
  A closed interval does not run on: the watch sold in 2029 did not keep
  reporting into 2030 merely because no line replaced it. Overlapping
  intervals for one origin are refused at validation, or a reading belongs to
  two instruments at once and the register answers ambiguously.
- Named `instruments`, not `devices`. `device` is already on every dataset and
  names the machine that WROTE THE LINE DOWN, which the schema deliberately
  keeps apart from the instrument that observed the value - conflating them is
  the false-corroboration defect. A `devices` dataset holding instruments
  would have manufactured the confound this one exists to remove.
- Resolved on `origin` alone. `source` is the channel a value arrived by, so
  the proposed `source or origin` fallback would have made a re-export - a
  channel change - resolve to a different instrument and read as an instrument
  change. 4331 of 9673 origin-bearing rows in the corpus name a channel and no
  instrument, so the fallback would have answered for all of them.
- Every field optional but the identity and the start, and nothing inferred.
  An unregistered origin resolves to nothing and renders exactly as it does
  today. `to_date` absent means still in use rather than unknown, so a
  register does not read a year later as a shelf of retired equipment.
- ines registers her kit, so the dataset ships exercised rather than empty.
  Her record already held the confound: 65.80 kg at the gym on 2030-05-30
  against 64.14 kg at home the same morning and 64.06 kg four days later, and
  nothing said those numbers came off different scales.
