- **P9a, the engine-side form of the client rule that a capability is not
  landed until something reaches for it** (#470). The client repository
  carries that rule; the engine did not, which is itself the defect it names -
  a rule applied to one of the two places it governs.
- **It is written down as UNENFORCED, with the reason**, because four
  mechanisms were measured and all four fail. In-repo reachability is
  manufactured: P9 requires a CLI harness, so 108 of 108 public surfaces have
  an in-repo caller by rule. Execution by a shipped path is over-inclusive:
  building the demo and rendering its rollup executes 13 of the 108, and the
  other 95 include `crossings`, `verdicts` and `goals`. A declared consumer
  converges on the safe answer, and here that is measured rather than
  predicted - the engine's one declaration of the form is false. And checking
  whether a surface's values appear in the rollup reports `questions` as
  rendering, because a date it carries appears elsewhere in the document.
- **No gate ships, and that is the finding rather than a gap.** A gate
  satisfiable without anything reaching for the surface would look like the
  rule and starve nothing, which is precisely what "a rule applied to one of
  the two places it names" produces. What the engine can enforce is narrower
  and different in kind: that it does not publish a claim about its consumers
  which it never checks.
